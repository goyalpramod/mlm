#### Manhattan distance 

This is a curious one. Imagine that you are in Manhattan right now. And you have to from point A to point B. Now you obviously cannot go through the buildings. 
So you must follow the roads built to the point you wish to go to. 

That is essentially what Manhattan distance is. It is the sum of the absolute differences of the coordinates. For two points (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂), it's |x₁ - x₂| + |y₁ - y₂|.
