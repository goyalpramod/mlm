Derivative

The idea of a derivative is very central to ML, it is often defined as the "Instantaneous rate of change". But that does not sound very simple nor does it seem easy to visualize. 
Sticking to our mantra, Let's first understand rate of change.

Lets say we have a simple line y = mx, If I asked you how much did the value of y changed as I moved from x=1 to x=3. 
It is quite easy to calculate as such. 

It's easy to see that how m = to our rate of change. M is known as the slope of the function. 

Now imagine if I have a more complex function 

Instead of asking how y changes as I move about x, what if I ask you. Depedning on where I currently am on the function, how do I know which direction to move to decrease my value. 

if we think of ourselves standing on top of the mountain blindfolded, a simple method can be to take a small step in all directions and move towards the one that decreases the value the most.
